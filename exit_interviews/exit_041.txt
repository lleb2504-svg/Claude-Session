Exit Interview — Meridian Technologies
Interview ID: EXIT-0041
Date: April 2025
Function: Sales
Role: Enterprise Account Executive
Location: NYC
Tenure: 3 years 2 months
Departure type: Voluntary

Notes:

Enterprise AE comp at Meridian is not competitive in the New York market anymore. I'm leaving for 35% more OTE. I want to be unromantic about it.

Specifics. My OTE here is $260K with a 50/50 split. Accelerators kick in at 110% and cap out at 200%. My new role is $350K OTE, 50/50 split, uncapped, with a stronger multiplier on overachievement. I would be leaving money on the table by staying.

I'm a bottom-of-the-funnel rep. I closed $4.2M in 2024. I am in the top quartile of AEs at Meridian by attainment. My base has gone up 4% total in the three years I've been here. Inflation alone erodes that. When I asked my manager about a market adjustment he said comp bands are set at the org level and the VP was not going to move them this year.

I understand the logic. I also understand that I can get more elsewhere. Both things are true.

What would have kept me: an uncapped plan and a base adjustment to account for three years of cost-of-living changes. Probably 20–25% more in total. That's a lot but I closed enough to pay for it ten times over.

I want to compliment a few things. The product is strong. The SE team I worked with is the best I have ever had. Sales ops is responsive. I would recommend Meridian to any AE who is in early-career and wants to learn. For late-career AEs, the comp compression against the market makes it hard.

One piece of advice for the next enterprise hire: negotiate hard upfront. The ceiling gets set on day one.
