Exit Interview — Meridian Technologies
Interview ID: EXIT-0012
Date: October 2024
Function: Engineering
Role: Software Engineer, Senior
Location: Remote-US
Tenure: 2 years 3 months
Departure type: Voluntary

Notes:

I'm burned out. Specifically, I'm on the Infrastructure team and our on-call rotation is not sustainable. Four engineers, 1 week primary every 4 weeks, 1 week secondary every 4 weeks, which means every other week you're tethered to a phone with 24/7 response obligations. We've lost two engineers to this rotation in the last 18 months. I'm the third.

The page volume has gotten worse over time. In 2023 my primary weeks averaged maybe 3-5 pages. In 2024 it's been 15-20 pages, some nights getting paged three times. We've been asking for staffing relief for a year. The usual answer is "we're hiring" but the roles either don't get approved or take a very long time to fill.

When I raised this with my manager in an emotional 1:1 in August, his response was "I hear you and I'm doing what I can." I believe him. I also know he has been telling leadership we are at critical staffing for 18 months and nothing has changed.

My wife and I had a real conversation last month. She said "if you get paged one more Sunday at 3am I'm going to answer the phone." She's right. I'm prioritizing my marriage over this rotation.

I'm joining a company where on-call is 1 week every 8 weeks with a proper incident commander rotation layered on top. It's not an ML role but I'll take boring infrastructure at a sustainable pace over exciting infrastructure on my current schedule.

The infra team at Meridian is great. The work is interesting. The staffing ratio is not sustainable and it will keep costing you senior engineers until it changes.
