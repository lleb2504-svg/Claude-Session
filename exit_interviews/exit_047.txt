Exit Interview — Meridian Technologies
Interview ID: EXIT-0047
Date: May 2025
Function: Data
Role: Data Scientist
Location: Remote-US
Tenure: 3 years 0 months
Departure type: Voluntary

Notes:

I'm going to be diplomatic but direct. My team had clear favorites and I was not one of them. I'm not sure if my manager was even aware of it, but the downstream effects were real.

The two engineers he came up with — literally worked with at his previous company — got the modeling projects. I got the data-quality and instrumentation work. That work is important and I did it well. But it is not how you get promoted as a data scientist at this company. When calibration came around, the modeling work is what the exec team remembered.

I want to be careful here. I am not saying my manager was malicious. I am saying he hired people from his old network, trusted them first, and the rest of us were on the outside of that trust. I saw it in 1:1 frequency, in who got the roadmap discussions, in who got picked for cross-functional collaboration.

When I raised this in my year-two review as "I'd like more visibility on modeling work," his response was "bring me a proposal." I brought two. Nothing happened. The third time I stopped.

I'm leaving for a different company where the DS team is smaller and less stratified. Comp is comparable. The deciding factor was that I interviewed with my would-be manager three times and she asked me in every conversation what I wanted to work on. My current manager has never asked me that in three years.

I'd also note that when I told my manager I was leaving, his first question was about the handoff, not about why I was leaving. Draw your own conclusions.
