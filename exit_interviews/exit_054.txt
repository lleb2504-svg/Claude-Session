Exit Interview — Meridian Technologies
Interview ID: EXIT-0054
Date: June 2025
Function: Product
Role: Product Manager, Senior
Location: SF
Tenure: 3 years 0 months
Departure type: Voluntary

Notes:

I had my first kid six months ago and the math on staying at Meridian stopped working. That's the honest answer.

PM work here is full-on. Meetings from 9 to 6, Slack runs late into the evening, and our PM culture leans toward "always be available." I was doing this before the baby and it was fine. Now I have a newborn and a partner who also has a demanding job and something had to give.

I asked my manager about going to four days a week or having a formal no-meetings window in the afternoons. The answer was "it'd be hard to make work in a PM role." I appreciate the honesty but that's also the problem. The PM role here is designed for someone without primary caregiver responsibilities. That's a lot of potential talent Meridian is locking out.

I'm joining a company that has a few senior PMs working four-day weeks and has made it a point of pride. Compensation is 15% lower. That's fine for now.

Other notes:

- My manager was supportive as a person. She tried to advocate for flex. The institutional answer was no.
- Parental leave was okay but returning to 100% capacity on day one didn't match my reality. A graduated return policy would have made a real difference.
- The childcare benefit pays for very little of actual childcare in the Bay Area. Consider whether it's still calibrated to market.

I'd come back in a few years if the role design flexes. I loved the product. I loved most of the team.

Please treat this as feedback from a happy-to-be-a-parent-now former PM. It is not a complaint. It is a diagnosis.
