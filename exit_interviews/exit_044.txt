Exit Interview — Meridian Technologies
Interview ID: EXIT-0044
Date: April 2025
Function: Operations
Role: Business Operations Manager, Senior
Location: Austin
Tenure: 6 years 2 months
Departure type: Voluntary

Notes:

I have a chronic health condition that has progressed and I need a simpler role with a lighter cognitive load. That's the core reason.

I've been at Meridian more than six years. Grew from an analyst into a senior manager. I want to be clear that I loved most of this job. My manager knew about my condition — I disclosed early — and has been accommodating. I don't have any complaint about how I've been treated.

The honest truth is that my role has gotten more complex over time and my capacity has gotten less. At some point those lines crossed. I was having more bad days than good. I was making mistakes I wouldn't have made three years ago. It wasn't fair to my team.

I'm moving to a smaller company where I'll be an individual contributor analyst. No people-management responsibilities. A clearly bounded scope. Less comp, much less stress.

A few things I'd like to pass along:

- The ADA / disability accommodation process at Meridian is genuinely good. I worked with People and they handled everything thoughtfully. Credit to the team.
- The insurance coverage has been life-changing for me. I want to mention that. Benefits matter.
- The culture of "always pushing" works for most people most of the time. It stops working when you have a condition that doesn't accommodate pushing. I didn't know how to ask for a slower lane.

Thank you for six years. This isn't a bitter exit. It's a calm one.
