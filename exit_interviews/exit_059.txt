Exit Interview — Meridian Technologies
Interview ID: EXIT-0059
Date: July 2025
Function: Engineering
Role: Software Engineer, Staff
Location: SF
Tenure: 4 years 4 months
Departure type: Voluntary

Notes:

Life event. My spouse got a job offer in Seattle that she couldn't turn down. We're moving. Meridian doesn't have a Seattle office and the role I'm in requires the kind of cross-functional coordination that doesn't work across timezones with a team that's mostly in SF.

I asked my manager about going fully remote from Seattle. We had a real conversation. He said it might work for a year or so but that the Staff+ track in my org assumes significant in-person collaboration and over time my trajectory would be affected. I appreciated the honesty.

I'm moving to a company headquartered in Seattle that's looking for Staff engineers. It's a lateral move in terms of scope. Slightly better comp. The main thing is the job being local.

I don't have complaints. I've had a great four years at Meridian. I shipped things I'm proud of. My managers have been solid. My peers are outstanding. The compensation has been fair. This is a life-stage change, not a grievance.

A few constructive thoughts since I'm here:

- If Meridian opens a Seattle office in the future, call me.
- The remote-work policy is inconsistent across orgs. Some managers are very flexible, others hold the in-office line. Unify this.
- My skip-level wrote me a really kind note when I gave notice. That mattered. More people should know how to do that.

Thanks for everything. I'll be a Meridian customer soon — my new company is one of your big accounts.
