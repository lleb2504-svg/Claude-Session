Exit Interview — Meridian Technologies
Interview ID: EXIT-0043
Date: April 2025
Function: Sales
Role: Account Executive, Senior
Location: NYC
Tenure: 3 years 3 months
Departure type: Voluntary

Notes:

I've wanted to move into the enterprise AE role for two years. I've consistently hit quota. I've closed some of the biggest logos in the mid-market book. When the enterprise segment opened two new territories last summer, I applied, and both roles went to external hires.

The explanation I got was that the enterprise team wanted "demonstrated enterprise experience." I do not have enterprise logo experience at Meridian specifically, because I've never been given enterprise territory. It's a chicken-and-egg problem. You have to already be Enterprise to get Enterprise, which means internal promotion into that tier is essentially impossible.

My manager agreed with my analysis. She said she'd raised it with the VP Sales multiple times. Nothing came of it.

I'm going to a competitor as an Enterprise AE. They gave me Enterprise territory and a ramp plan. They believed I could do the job based on my mid-market track record. Apparently that's not a crazy leap.

One piece of broader feedback: internal mobility in Sales at Meridian is brittle. SDR-to-AE is paused. Mid-Market-to-Enterprise is de facto closed. The only way up is out and then back in, or laterally to a different company.

I'll miss the team. I'll miss the customers. I'm frustrated that a 12-month internal move would have kept me here and the company chose not to make that move.

Final note: my replacement will have to be hired externally. Based on current recruiting cycles that's 3–4 months plus 6 months of ramp. My book will be undermanaged for most of the second half of the year. I flagged this in February.
