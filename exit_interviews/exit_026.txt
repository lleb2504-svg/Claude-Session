Exit Interview — Meridian Technologies
Interview ID: EXIT-0026
Date: January 2025
Function: Product
Role: Product Manager, Senior
Location: SF
Tenure: 5 years 2 months
Departure type: Voluntary

Notes:

Leaving because I've been blocked at Staff PM for three cycles in a row and I've lost faith that it will happen.

First cycle: I was told I wasn't ready because I hadn't owned a standalone product area yet. That made sense. I took on the billing product area.

Second cycle: I was told I wasn't ready because I hadn't demonstrated "strategic influence" at the exec level. I led the pricing-rewrite proposal to the CEO, including the business case, the phased rollout, and the customer research. It was approved and shipped.

Third cycle: I was told the Staff bar had "shifted" and that I was "very close" but needed more time. No specific gap was named.

At that point I stopped asking questions and started updating my LinkedIn.

I am moving to a late-stage startup as a Group PM. The title is above Staff at Meridian. The comp is comparable. The ceiling is higher because they're growing faster than we are.

Feedback for the next CHRO listening:

- When the bar keeps shifting, people assume it's personal. Sometimes it is, sometimes it isn't. The perception kills you either way.
- The Staff PM layer at Meridian is extremely stable. I looked at the org chart. The same six people have been at Staff PM for three years. That's a long wait for those of us below it.
- My manager was fine. She tried. The calibration process above her was not coherent.

I want to leave on good terms. I was proud to work on the pricing rewrite, which is still paying dividends. I hope that counts toward my legacy here.
