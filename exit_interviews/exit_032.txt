Exit Interview — Meridian Technologies
Interview ID: EXIT-0032
Date: February 2025
Function: Engineering
Role: Software Engineer, Staff
Location: SF
Tenure: 3 years 2 months
Departure type: Voluntary

Notes:

Third infrastructure engineer leaving from my team in under a year. I don't want to belabor what others have probably told you: the on-call is unsustainable, the platform has grown faster than the team, and management has not been able to get staffing relief.

What I want to add is the quieter consequence, which is that I haven't done meaningful new engineering work in over a year. Every cycle my "roadmap" gets eaten by reactive incident work, post-mortems, mitigation PRs, and escalations. I was hired to architect the next generation of our data plane. I haven't touched it. Instead I've been duct-taping the current generation.

This degrades my skills over time. A Staff engineer who spends a year in firefighting mode loses the parts of the job that make the next Staff role possible. I need to get back into design work while I'm still current.

I'm joining a company as a Staff engineer on a platform team that just finished a similar hyper-growth phase and is now in architecture mode. I'll spend the next six months writing docs and prototypes. That's what I need.

Some additional things to flag:
- The infra team has had no backfill for the two engineers who left before me. The remaining two people will be in 1-week-on, 1-week-off rotation, which is not legal in some jurisdictions and not humane anywhere.
- Our best SRE candidates during the last round went to competitors because we couldn't match comp.
- I'd suggest escalating this above the VP level. Our team's attrition is a leading indicator.

I liked working here when we had time to do it well.
