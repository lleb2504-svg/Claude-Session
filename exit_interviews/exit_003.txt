Exit Interview — Meridian Technologies
Interview ID: EXIT-0003
Date: August 2024
Function: Design
Role: Product Designer, Senior
Location: Remote-US
Tenure: 2 years 5 months
Departure type: Voluntary

Notes:

I have been working overtime consistently for the better part of a year and it is finally catching up with me physically. I have had migraines most weeks for three months. I'm 33.

The design team at Meridian has a couple of structural issues that create the overwork. First, each designer owns too many product surfaces. I had three major product areas. Anyone of them was a full-time job. All three together was unreasonable. Second, design gets looped into Product cycles late, which means we do a lot of work at the end under time pressure rather than through the cycle with breathing room. Third, the design ops function that would help us prioritize doesn't exist.

I flagged the workload six months ago. My manager was sympathetic and we had a real conversation. The outcome was supposed to be reducing my surface coverage. That happened for about four weeks. Then a peer left the company, some of her work came to me, and I was back at three surfaces.

I'm moving to a company where designers own one surface at a time. Comp is slightly lower. I'll take the trade.

Feedback:
- Meridian should set a designer-to-product-surface ratio and defend it. Right now we over-commit by default.
- The Design org attrition this year has been heavily concentrated in senior ICs. Talk to your design leads about why.
- Backfilling through internal redistribution of work is a short-term hack. It accelerates the attrition.

Thanks for two good years. I hope the design team figures this out.
