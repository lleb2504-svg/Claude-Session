Exit Interview — Meridian Technologies
Interview ID: EXIT-0013
Date: October 2024
Function: Sales
Role: Account Executive
Location: Austin
Tenure: 4 years 0 months
Departure type: Voluntary

Notes:

Going through a divorce and need a fresh start. I considered staying and just trying to power through but my therapist was pretty direct with me about the value of a clean break. I'm moving out of Austin and taking a role somewhere new.

This is a personal departure. Meridian has been a steady employer and my sales career grew here from an SDR up to a senior AE. My tenure has been good. My manager knows the situation and has been kind.

I want to use this space to say something a little broader: Meridian's employee assistance program was helpful to me this year. I didn't know what to expect when I called. The counselor was professional, took me seriously, and helped me find a good therapist in Austin. That resource is undersold and I think more people should know about it. Maybe that's a note for People.

In terms of career at Meridian: I've had a fine ride. Comp has been competitive enough. My book has grown. I've closed enough to feel good about my record. My manager this year has been better than most. No complaints on the business side.

I'm joining a different SaaS company in a different city. Similar role, similar comp. Different life.

If there were an option to return in two years when the dust settles, I'd take it seriously. I'm not burning bridges and I don't think I have a lot of them to burn.

Thank you to the friends I'm leaving behind.
