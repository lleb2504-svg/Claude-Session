Exit Interview — Meridian Technologies
Interview ID: EXIT-0024
Date: January 2025
Function: Product
Role: Product Manager
Location: SF
Tenure: 2 years 2 months
Departure type: Voluntary

Notes:

My manager micromanaged me into the ground and I don't think she even realized she was doing it. She is a smart product leader, good instincts, but she cannot delegate. Every PRD I wrote came back with her rewrites. Every customer conversation I had, she wanted a recap the same day. After 18 months of this I stopped having original ideas because they were going to be overwritten anyway.

I would love to separate this feedback into "things I think Meridian should fix" and "things that are my own fault." Here's my list.

Meridian could fix: a PM manager being too hands-on is not a new problem in this industry and there should be coaching for new product leaders on how to let go. I don't think my manager got that coaching. It showed.

My own fault: I did not escalate. I was afraid of looking like I couldn't handle feedback. By the time I had the courage to talk to my skip-level I had already decided to leave.

What I loved: the product surface I was working on, my eng partners, and the customers. Honestly our customers at Meridian are some of the kindest and most engaged I have ever worked with. That's not nothing.

What I'm doing next: joining a Series B company as their first PM. It's a risk but I am excited to own the entire product. If it doesn't work out, I will probably come back to a company like Meridian — ideally Meridian itself if there's the right team.

Ask me in two years whether I made the right call. Today I am 80% sure.
