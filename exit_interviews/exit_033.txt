Exit Interview — Meridian Technologies
Interview ID: EXIT-0033
Date: February 2025
Function: Finance
Role: Finance Business Partner
Location: NYC
Tenure: 5 years 3 months
Departure type: Voluntary

Notes:

My father had a stroke last year and my family needs me back home in a small town in western Pennsylvania to help manage his care. Meridian doesn't have operations anywhere near there and the FBP role requires enough synchronous collaboration with the GTM leadership team that fully remote from a rural setting doesn't work.

My manager and I explored everything. A remote FBP arrangement. A reduced-hours role. A short sabbatical. None of them fit. The job as designed needs someone who can be in meetings with east-coast and west-coast GTM leaders most weekdays, and my new reality is that I need flexibility during business hours for medical appointments.

I'm taking a remote controller role at a much smaller company where the compensation is lower but the job is literally designed for someone with my constraints. I looked for two months before finding it.

I want to say publicly (to whatever internal record this goes into): my manager was extraordinary throughout this. She offered me a leave of absence. She was willing to restructure the role. She helped me think through alternatives without any pressure about the outcome. She is a model of what a manager should be when an employee has a hard life event.

I'd also say: the bereavement and caregiver-leave policies at Meridian are decent. Not great. A few additional weeks of paid caregiver leave would have made the decision harder for me, which is my point — it would have helped retain me.

Five years here was a gift. I'm grateful. I hope to stay in touch.
