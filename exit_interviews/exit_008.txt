Exit Interview — Meridian Technologies
Interview ID: EXIT-0008
Date: September 2024
Function: Engineering
Role: Software Engineer, Staff
Location: SF
Tenure: 5 years 0 months
Departure type: Voluntary

Notes:

Starting my own company. My co-founder and I have been working on this for 18 months on weekends and it's gotten real enough that we need to commit.

This is the best moment of my career and I'm genuinely thrilled. I also want to leave with a generous spirit toward Meridian because it's given me the runway and experience to take this leap.

What Meridian gave me:
- Five years of tenure at a well-respected company, which investors will look at favorably.
- A group of exceptional colleagues who I'll likely work with again.
- Equity that vested enough to give me a year of savings to bootstrap on.
- A skip-level who taught me more about engineering leadership than anyone else I've worked for. I'm grateful.

What I'd constructively flag on my way out:

- The Staff+ engineer population has been shrinking this year through departures. I'm one of them. If I were CHRO I'd run the numbers on your Staff+ attrition by quarter for the last four quarters. The curve is steeper than it should be.
- Tenured engineers are being asked to do more manager-adjacent work (roadmap writing, cross-team coordination, mentoring) without a corresponding compensation adjustment. For a while that's fine. At the Staff+ level it eventually triggers "why am I doing the manager's job at IC pay."
- The founder-friendly exit process here has been smooth. Credit to the People team.

Keep an eye on my company. I hope to sell to Meridian as a customer in two years. If we get there, it'll be a nice circle to close.

Onward.
