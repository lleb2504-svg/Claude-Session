Exit Interview — Meridian Technologies
Interview ID: EXIT-0051
Date: June 2025
Function: Engineering
Role: Software Engineer, Senior
Location: SF
Tenure: 4 years 2 months
Departure type: Voluntary

Notes:

I'm leaving for comp and I want to explain why specifically.

Six months ago I was promoted to Senior SWE, which I'd been pushing for for a year. The increase was $12K on my base. I was happy in the moment. Then two things happened.

First, I got recruited by a competitor and their first offer out of the gate was meaningfully higher than my new base — not a huge margin but meaningful. I didn't accept; I told my manager. He asked what I wanted to stay and I said "market adjust my base." He came back with $5K.

Second, I learned by comparing notes with two male peers at my level that they were paid $10–15K more than me. Same level. Similar tenure. Same org. One of them had joined a year after me. My manager's response when I brought this up was "comp is based on a lot of factors and I can't share specifics." Fine, don't share. Fix it.

I took the competitor offer. I went back once more to let my manager counter. He did — close to the competitor's number. I asked him the obvious question: why didn't you pay me this before I got an outside offer? He didn't have a good answer.

This isn't only about me. I have talked to other women at Meridian in engineering and we all have a version of this story. You are paying us less than our male peers and the only way to fix it is to force you to by getting an outside offer. Most women I know don't want to go through that process. They just leave.

Please look at your pay data. It's in your data.
