Exit Interview — Meridian Technologies
Interview ID: EXIT-0018
Date: November 2024
Function: Product
Role: Product Manager, Staff
Location: SF
Tenure: 5 years 0 months
Departure type: Voluntary

Notes:

Accepted a VP of Product role at a Series B company. This is a step up in scope that would have taken 2-3 more years at Meridian and wasn't guaranteed.

I want to reflect briefly on the five years because they were good.

I joined as a Senior PM. I promoted to Staff two years in. I owned the pricing and packaging product area for the last three years, which I think drove real revenue impact. I worked with three different engineering partners over that time. All good.

The reason I'm taking the VP role now is that it's the right career move. The Series B company has real product-market fit, a fast-growing revenue base, and a talented small team. Going in at VP means I'll shape the org rather than inherit it. That's the kind of opportunity that comes up rarely.

Honest observations about the Product function at Meridian:

- PM leadership is a bit top-heavy. There are good paths up to Staff. Beyond Staff the slots are limited. I'd have been waiting in line for a year or two.
- The Group PM layer has been growing, which is healthy. Something between Staff and Director would have been a useful bridge for me.
- Our PMM partnership has improved significantly this year. Credit to the new CMO.

Thank you to my manager, my skip, and especially the two PMs I mentored. I'm proud of the work we did together.

I hope to be an alum, a reference, and a future customer.
