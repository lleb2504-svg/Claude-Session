Exit Interview — Meridian Technologies
Interview ID: EXIT-0058
Date: July 2025
Function: Engineering
Role: Software Engineer, Staff
Location: Remote-US
Tenure: 5 years 1 month
Departure type: Voluntary

Notes:

Equity is the short answer. I joined Meridian at Series C and took a base cut for a decent equity package. The company has been "about to IPO" for three years now. Meanwhile my strike price has crept up in refreshes and the actual IPO hasn't happened.

I did the math with my spouse and realized that I've been effectively paid base only for two years. My refreshes at year three and four were 30% of what new hires at my level got. They call it "front-loaded" and technically it is, but the real story is that comp committee is prioritizing new hire retention over tenured-employee retention. I get the logic. I don't have to like it.

When I raised this with my manager he said he'd talk to his VP. What came back was a one-time equity top-up worth about a quarter of what I would have made at a pre-IPO competitor in the same role. I took the competitor offer.

Other things I want to note:
- My manager was fine. Supportive, let me run my team, advocated for my promotion. This is not a manager issue.
- My team is fine. I like the engineers I work with. Several have told me they are looking too.
- The product is fine. Growth story still makes sense to me.

The issue is purely that tenured employees at the Staff+ level are effectively bearing the cost of Meridian staying private longer than expected. Either IPO or refresh aggressively enough to compensate for the delay.

I'll consult during my transition. I want to leave the handoff clean. Not angry, just moving on.
