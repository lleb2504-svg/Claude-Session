Exit Interview — Meridian Technologies
Interview ID: EXIT-0049
Date: May 2025
Function: Engineering
Role: Software Engineer II
Location: Austin
Tenure: 1 year 5 months
Departure type: Voluntary

Notes:

I've been on the infrastructure team for about a year and I'm already leaving. The on-call rotation is brutal and the way people above me talk about it makes me think it's not going to change.

I joined Meridian out of a grad program because I wanted to do systems work and I was told this team did great work. The work is great when we're not firefighting. We are firefighting constantly.

In my one year I've been on primary 13 times. I've lost count of after-hours pages. Last month I had four nights in a row where I was paged between 1am and 4am. I'm 26 years old and I feel physically worn down.

I talked to my manager in February. He told me to take a few days off and said he was pushing for staffing. I took three days off. I came back and we were down a person because a teammate had resigned. Things got worse.

I'm joining a company where I'll be on an application team, not infrastructure. I'm taking a slight pay cut. It's worth it.

What would have kept me: 24-month commitment to grow the team to at least 6 primary on-call rotations (i.e., 1 week every 6), and a temporary reduction in project commitments until we got there. Neither was offered.

I want to flag the youngest engineers on this team specifically because we don't have the political capital to push back. When my manager schedules me for an extra on-call week to cover someone's vacation, I say yes. I wish I hadn't.

Good luck to the remaining folks on the team. I mean that.
