Exit Interview — Meridian Technologies
Interview ID: EXIT-0015
Date: November 2024
Function: Product
Role: Product Manager, Senior
Location: SF
Tenure: 3 years 6 months
Departure type: Voluntary

Notes:

The bonus I got in February was genuinely disappointing and it broke something for me. Let me explain.

Our PM team had a huge year. We shipped the pricing redesign on schedule, launched the enterprise tier ahead of schedule, and the re-architecture of the onboarding funnel landed in Q4 and has already moved conversion metrics. I ran two of those three projects. I thought I had a case for a strong rating and a strong bonus.

My rating was "Often Exceeds." Good rating. My bonus was 90% of target. Target for my role is 15% of base. I got 13.5% of base. For context my comp is $205K base, so we're talking $27,675 before tax versus the $30,750 I expected.

Small number in absolute terms. Big number in trust terms. The delta wasn't what broke me — it was the mismatch between the rating and the payout. If I Often Exceed, why am I below target? When I asked, the answer was "the bonus pool is sized to corporate performance and we didn't hit the corporate goal." Fine. Say that. Don't give me a rating that says I exceeded expectations and then pay me as if I didn't.

I interviewed at three places. Two offered PM manager roles. I'm taking the one where the comp is more transparent — they publish the bonus-pool sizing and the calibration rubric. I want to work in a place where I can trust the math.

I like a lot about Meridian. My manager, my team, the problem space. If you fix the variable-comp transparency I would recommend the place. Until then people who hit Often Exceeds and don't get paid for it will leave.
