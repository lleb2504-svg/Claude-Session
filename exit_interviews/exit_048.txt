Exit Interview — Meridian Technologies
Interview ID: EXIT-0048
Date: May 2025
Function: Product
Role: Product Manager
Location: SF
Tenure: 4 years 2 months (joined via Finch acquisition, 2022)
Departure type: Voluntary

Notes:

I came to Meridian through the Finch acquisition in 2022. I've been a PM on the same product surface ever since. I came in as a Senior PM at my old company and got re-leveled down at the deal close. I was told that was temporary and that once I had some context at Meridian I'd be back to Senior.

Three years later I'm still not Senior. Every cycle I get told I'm "close" or "next time." Every cycle someone hired externally gets slotted in above me. I don't think my manager is doing this on purpose. I think there's a quiet policy that says acquired employees should prove themselves again, and no one will say that out loud but that's how it plays out.

I have talked to four or five other former Finch folks and we all have versions of this story. People who came in as Staff-level stayed at Senior. People who came in as Senior stayed at Mid. A couple of us have been promoted but it's rare enough that we notice when it happens and name it — "so-and-so got through."

I'm joining a company where my title will match my experience and my starting offer will match market for that experience. Neither of those was true at Meridian.

Things I liked: the product surface I worked on was interesting. My direct peers were great. I made friends.

Things I'd change: actually retaining the acquired cohort requires being deliberate about career progression for them. It doesn't happen on its own. Two years out from the deal I don't think Meridian ever did the work.

Please ask the former-Finch folks individually how they've experienced the three years. You'll hear a consistent story.
