Exit Interview — Meridian Technologies
Interview ID: EXIT-0034
Date: February 2025
Function: Sales
Role: Account Executive, Senior
Location: Remote-US
Tenure: 3 years 1 month
Departure type: Voluntary

Notes:

External offer from a high-growth SaaS competitor. Better comp, better territory, faster sales cycles. I'm taking it.

Specifics since this is sales:

- My OTE here is $230K with a 60/40 split. My new OTE is $300K with a 50/50 split. More upside, same floor.
- My current territory is mature. Renewal-heavy, not a lot of net-new. The new territory is greenfield enterprise — harder work in the short term, bigger upside in 18 months.
- Our sales cycle here has stretched noticeably as we've moved up-market. Average is 9 months now. The new company sells to mid-market with 3-4 month cycles. Fundamentally different rhythm.

Feedback:

- Mid-market sales at Meridian has become enterprise-adjacent without changing the titling or quota structure. My quota has gone up but my deals have gotten harder to close in the same timeframe. The math gets brittle.
- AE attrition in my segment is high this year. Talk to the other AEs who have left in the last two quarters — you'll hear similar themes.
- My manager has been great. She tried to get me to stay. The structural comp issue was above her.

I've liked working here. The team is sharp. The product is strong. The cycle has just shifted and I need to shift with it.
