Exit Interview — Meridian Technologies
Interview ID: EXIT-0001
Date: August 2024
Function: Customer Success
Role: Customer Success Manager
Location: London
Tenure: 1 year 7 months
Departure type: Voluntary

Notes:

I'm leaving because of my manager and I want to be honest about that. She is not a bad person. She is a bad manager for me. There's a difference.

Background: I joined to own our enterprise EMEA book. My manager runs CS for the region and has been doing CS leadership for 15 years. She has strong opinions about how CS should be done. I have strong opinions too. That's not a bad combination in principle. In practice it became a problem because she wouldn't let me run my book my way.

Example. I had a customer renewal that was going sideways. I thought we should escalate to the customer's exec sponsor and have our CEO send a note. My manager said that was "too aggressive" for EMEA. We lost the renewal. Three months later she sent exactly that CEO note to a different customer in a similar situation. I asked her what changed. She said "the context was different." I could not tell you what about the context was different.

This is one example. There were many.

I also want to flag that several of my colleagues in London CS have left or are looking in the last six months. You will see the attrition data and know this already. Before you conclude that this is all manager issues, some of it is the return-to-office push and frankly the hours we work compared to the US team. But the manager issue is real and compounds the rest.

I'm going to a competitor. Not for comp (it's about the same). For autonomy. I hope Meridian can fix this — I believed in the product and still do.
