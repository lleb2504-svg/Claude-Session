Exit Interview — Meridian Technologies
Interview ID: EXIT-0021
Date: December 2024
Function: Customer Success
Role: Customer Success Manager, Senior
Location: Austin
Tenure: 3 years 7 months
Departure type: Voluntary

Notes:

I wanted to make the lateral move from CS to Product Management and Meridian wouldn't let me.

For two years I've been making the case that my customer relationships, domain knowledge, and sense for what we should build would translate well to a PM role. I've shadowed PMs. I've written a couple of PRDs that they used almost verbatim. I did not just raise my hand out of nowhere.

The answer has been consistently: "PMs are hired with PM experience" and "we've not historically done CS-to-PM transitions." I offered to interview through the normal PM hiring process. I was told that would be awkward for the hiring manager and I should apply externally if I wanted to be a PM, then apply back to Meridian later.

So that's what I'm doing. I'm joining a smaller company as a PM directly. In 18 months I'll have the PM experience Meridian wanted me to have. Whether I come back is an open question.

The ironic thing is that my CS account knowledge was the reason the PM team brought me into design conversations in the first place. That knowledge is going out the door with me.

Additional notes:

- I'm not angry. I'm disappointed.
- CS at Meridian is good work but it has limited vertical ceiling and very limited lateral options. You lose your most growth-oriented CSMs because of this.
- My manager tried. My skip tried. The blocker was in the PM org, and it was cultural, not individual.

I want to thank the customers who championed me internally. They know who they are.
