Exit Interview — Meridian Technologies
Interview ID: EXIT-0035
Date: March 2025
Function: Engineering
Role: Software Engineer II
Location: Austin
Tenure: 2 years 1 month
Departure type: Voluntary

Notes:

Honestly I'm burnt out and I don't think I'd be leaving if I had a different manager. I want to say that clearly because I've heard other engineers on my team say the same thing and I don't know if anyone's listening.

My manager runs 1:1s like status updates. Every week the same questions: what did you ship, what are you blocked on, when will X be done. There's no space to talk about how I'm doing. I asked for skip-level conversations twice and both times he told me I could go but it "wouldn't look great." I stopped asking.

Last quarter he added me to a project that was already behind and said it was a "stretch opportunity." It wasn't. It was a death march that had eaten two other engineers before me. When we missed the deadline he told leadership it was because I "still had ramp-up to do" — I have been here two years. I overheard that from someone in the hallway after the review.

I'm going to a smaller company where I know my manager because I interviewed with her twice. The comp is actually slightly less. I just want to work for someone who thinks I'm worth investing in.

A couple of things that would have kept me: a real path to Senior SWE, an honest conversation about what I need to work on, and being moved to a different team before I decided to leave. I asked HR about a transfer six months ago and never heard back. That one stung.

I'd like to stay in touch with a few of the folks I worked with. Please pass along my contact info to my skip.
