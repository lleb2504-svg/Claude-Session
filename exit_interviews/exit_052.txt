Exit Interview — Meridian Technologies
Interview ID: EXIT-0052
Date: June 2025
Function: Finance
Role: Finance Business Partner, Senior
Location: SF
Tenure: 4 years 4 months
Departure type: Voluntary

Notes:

Equity refresh cycle didn't work out for me and I can't justify staying for another year to see if it will.

When I joined Meridian I was told that the company refreshes equity at the three-year mark to make sure tenured employees don't fall off an equity cliff. At my three-year I got a refresh that was about 40% of what I expected. When I asked why, the answer was that my role "doesn't have the same equity intensity as revenue-adjacent roles." This is news to me because my original offer was structured at parity with revenue-adjacent roles at my level.

So either the offer calculation changed or the refresh policy is different from the initial offer philosophy. Neither is fine, honestly.

I'm joining a pre-IPO competitor as a finance lead with a meaningful equity package. My base is 8% higher. The equity is the real difference — roughly 3x what I'd have earned at Meridian at the current refresh rate. If the competitor goes public at anything like their rumored valuation I'll be very glad I made the move.

Other notes:

- I enjoyed working with most of the people at Meridian and I want that on record. This is a comp decision, not a people decision.
- The finance org has been restructured three times in four years. That's harder than people realize. I have had four managers. Each re-org cost me a quarter of ramp.
- Succession on my team is not great. My departure leaves one of the GTM business units without a finance partner for at least two months. Sorry about that. I mentioned it in July last year when I raised the refresh conversation. It was not treated as urgent at the time.

Thank you for four years.
