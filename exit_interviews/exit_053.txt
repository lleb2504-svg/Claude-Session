Exit Interview — Meridian Technologies
Interview ID: EXIT-0053
Date: June 2025
Function: Operations
Role: Operations Manager (joined via Finch acquisition)
Location: Dublin
Tenure: 2 years 8 months
Departure type: Voluntary

Notes:

I came in through the Finch deal. I want to be honest about what it's been like. It has felt, continuously, like being a second-class citizen at Meridian.

Specific examples:

When the all-hands presentations happen, the speakers talk about "Meridian's history" starting in 2018. The Finch team's work before the acquisition is not acknowledged. A small thing, but it accumulates.

When re-orgs happen, the former-Finch employees are always the ones who get re-mapped. I have had four managers in 32 months. Two of my Finch peers have had five.

When promotions happen, the former-Finch folks I started with have been promoted at about half the rate of the Meridian-original employees in the same office. I've tracked this informally.

When the global leadership visits Dublin, they don't visit the Finch-legacy parts of the office. I know that sounds petty. It isn't petty when it happens every visit for three years.

I wanted to stay. I had good relationships with my Dublin peers, both Finch-legacy and Meridian-original. The work was interesting. But I kept waiting to be fully folded in, and it never happened, and I stopped waiting.

I'm joining a company where I'll be a full member of the team on day one. Comp is comparable. Level is a step up.

Request: please look at the former-Finch cohort as a cohort. Compare their promo rates, their attrition, their engagement scores to the broader Meridian population. I'd bet the numbers will tell a story and it's a story I've tried to tell for three years.
