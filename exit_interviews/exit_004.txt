Exit Interview — Meridian Technologies
Interview ID: EXIT-0004
Date: September 2024
Function: Engineering
Role: Software Engineer II
Location: Austin
Tenure: 1 year 2 months
Departure type: Voluntary

Notes:

Short tenure, I know. Please still take this seriously.

I was hired into my manager's team and within six months realized that the way he communicates was not going to work for me. He would tell me one thing in 1:1 and tell the team a different thing in standup. I would ask clarifying questions and he'd be annoyed. I would match his delivery style to what the team was told and he'd pull me aside to say that's not what he meant.

I am a pretty direct communicator. I need clarity to do my job. I don't need my manager to be perfect but I need him to be consistent. He was not.

When I asked to transfer teams at the eight-month mark I was told I was too early in my ramp-up. I was doing fine work by that point. Three months later I asked again. I was told there were no open req's on the teams I wanted. A month after that a peer on my team moved to the team I had asked about. So the req was open. Nobody told me.

I'm leaving for a startup and I expect I will regret it in 18 months when the startup fails and I could have been at Meridian three years in. But I'll take that risk over one more performance review with this manager.

If there's a way to give your engineers a real lateral-move option before they resign, please build it. I would have stayed. My skip-level told me the same when I gave notice. "I wish I had known sooner." Me too.
