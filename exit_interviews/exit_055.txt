Exit Interview — Meridian Technologies
Interview ID: EXIT-0055
Date: June 2025
Function: Design
Role: Product Designer, Senior
Location: NYC
Tenure: 3 years 3 months
Departure type: Voluntary

Notes:

I'm leaving tech. Joining a high school as a computer science and design teacher. This has been a slow transition — I've been teaching part-time on weekends for about a year through a local nonprofit — and I've decided I want to do it full-time.

Nothing about Meridian is driving this. My role was fine. My manager was fine. My pay was fine. I just discovered that working with teenagers is more meaningful to me than shipping the next B2B feature.

A few thoughts since I'm filling out the interview:

- I will be taking a significant pay cut. Teachers are not compensated remotely like tech workers. If Meridian has any kind of volunteering or nonprofit partnership program that could route tech workers into education, I'd be happy to help shape it. I learned a lot at Meridian that would be useful to young designers and engineers.
- The design team has been a highlight. I want to name my manager specifically — she was a great model for what design leadership looks like. I'll miss working with her.
- The recent design-ops hiring has made things a lot better than they were 18 months ago. Good move.

I don't have constructive criticism to offer. I'm leaving to do something different, not to escape something wrong.

Two asks:

- Keep me on the Meridian design-community Slack if you have one after the fact. I'd like to stay connected.
- If any Meridian designers want to do a "teaching day" at my new school, I'd love to host.

Thanks for three good years.
