Exit Interview — Meridian Technologies
Interview ID: EXIT-0010
Date: October 2024
Function: Design
Role: Product Designer, Senior
Location: SF
Tenure: 2 years 4 months
Departure type: Voluntary

Notes:

Leaving for comp. I interviewed at three places out of curiosity and all three offered me 20%+ more base. The one I took is $28K more, with better equity and a signing bonus.

I don't think Meridian has calibrated designer comp to market. Last year engineers at my level got solid annual increases. Designers got 2–3%. We're in the same org, work on the same products, have the same impact on outcomes. The pay bands diverged.

I raised this with my manager twice. The answer was "design comp is benchmarked against other design teams, not against engineering." I understand that's the standard approach. It doesn't match how designers at our tier get recruited. The companies offering me more are not benchmarking against other design teams. They are benchmarking against engineering because they want design and engineering to be peer-leveled.

My manager was good. I liked my team. I had interesting work. I am leaving because the financial math wasn't working over a three-to-five year horizon.

Secondary comments:

- The onboarding for new designers is pretty rough. I spent the first month figuring out what tools we used and who owned which surfaces. A design ops function would help.
- Design reviews are a highlight. I learned a lot from the leads in my org.
- The promo process for design is opaque. I was told I was "not quite ready" for Staff and given no actionable feedback on what "ready" looked like.

I would come back if the comp structure changed. I left on good terms and I mean it.
