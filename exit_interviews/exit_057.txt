Exit Interview — Meridian Technologies
Interview ID: EXIT-0057
Date: July 2025
Function: Engineering
Role: Software Engineer, Senior
Location: Remote-US
Tenure: 4 years 0 months
Departure type: Voluntary

Notes:

I'll keep this short because I've written a long version in email to my skip-level already. The TL;DR is that the manager I had for the last 18 months never once advocated for me and I'm done.

Specifics. I didn't get promoted to Staff despite shipping the auth rewrite and leading the on-call improvement initiative. I asked him what was missing and he told me to "be more visible." I asked what that looked like and he said "write more design docs." I wrote more design docs. The next cycle he said I needed to "expand my scope." I was already on-call lead for the org. When I pointed that out he said "oh right, I forgot."

He forgot. That's the quote.

I was the most tenured engineer on the team. I was mentoring four juniors and two senior hires. I know my impact. When my skip-level asked me during an exec-visibility program what I wanted, I told him I wanted to be promoted. That was in February. In April I got the same "Consistently Meets." I don't know what else to do.

What I loved about Meridian: the platform engineering team as a whole, the on-call culture after the rewrite, genuinely smart colleagues, and a codebase I'm proud of. I would recommend this place to an engineer who gets placed on any team except this one.

If you want to retain senior engineers, the manager layer in platform eng is where you should focus. Ask around. I'm not the only one with this story.
