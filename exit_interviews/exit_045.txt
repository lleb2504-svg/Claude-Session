Exit Interview — Meridian Technologies
Interview ID: EXIT-0045
Date: April 2025
Function: Engineering
Role: Software Engineer, Senior
Location: SF
Tenure: 3 years 3 months
Departure type: Voluntary

Notes:

External offer from a late-stage private company that's well-positioned for IPO. Title is Staff, which I've been trying to reach here for over a year. Equity is meaningfully better. Base is slightly higher.

I want to be clear about the order of events. I wasn't looking actively. A founder of the company reached out to me on LinkedIn and pitched an interesting project. I interviewed out of curiosity. Their process was fast and the offer was strong enough that I felt I had to take it seriously.

I took the offer to my manager. His counter got close on base but couldn't match the level promotion. The level mattered because the scope at the new company is Staff-level work from day one, and my current work at Meridian hasn't quite been Staff-scope despite me pushing for it.

So: I'm leaving less for money and more for the level and the scope that come with the level.

What I'd note on my way out:

- Counter-offers at Meridian tend to focus on base. When the underlying issue is level and scope, base alone doesn't close the gap.
- The Staff promotion conversation at Meridian is extended. If a candidate is perceived as ready but the slot isn't open, "stay for another cycle" works only up to a point.
- My team and manager have been great. If I could clone the team at a different company with more Staff slots, I would have stayed.

Thanks for three years. This has been the best job I've had so far and I'm taking the next one in the same spirit.
