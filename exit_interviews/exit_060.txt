Exit Interview — Meridian Technologies
Interview ID: EXIT-0060
Date: July 2025
Function: Data
Role: Data Scientist
Location: SF
Tenure: 2 years 2 months
Departure type: Voluntary

Notes:

Starting a PhD in computational social science in the fall. I applied last year and was admitted to my top choice. This is a long-standing academic interest of mine — I finished a research master's before taking my industry job at Meridian and always intended to go back.

This is not a Meridian-driven departure in any way. My work here has been excellent training. I'd recommend the DS team at Meridian to anyone.

Brief reflections:

- The quality of DS work here is high. I published two blog posts and one internal whitepaper that I will reference in my PhD applications.
- My manager supported my applications with a thoughtful recommendation letter. I really appreciated that — a lot of managers would have seen the applications as a retention threat.
- The DS-to-research pipeline is interesting. I'm aware of at least four Meridian DSs who've left for PhDs in the last three years. Maybe there's an alumni network to keep.

Constructive things I'd flag since I'm writing:

- The DS onboarding pathway could be better. When I joined I was on three projects within my first month, which was too many. A focused first-project approach would have ramped me faster.
- Data infrastructure for DS work is better than it was when I joined. Credit to the ML platform team.
- The career ladder for DS at Meridian is clearer than for MLE. The MLE ladder is vague, which I think contributes to the higher MLE attrition I've observed.

Thanks for a good two years. I'll be at Meridian's annual data conference as a presenter in a few years if all goes well.
