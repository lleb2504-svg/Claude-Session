Exit Interview — Meridian Technologies
Interview ID: EXIT-0029
Date: February 2025
Function: Sales
Role: Account Executive, Senior
Location: NYC
Tenure: 2 years 6 months
Departure type: Voluntary

Notes:

My manager promised me a promotion to Enterprise AE at the start of last year and spent the rest of the year explaining why it wasn't happening yet. I blew quota in 2023. I blew quota in 2024. By the time I realized the promotion was never going to happen I had already missed a couple of windows to leave, which is on me.

His usual line was "we need one more quarter of consistency." I had five quarters of consistency. Then it was "we need you to close a marquee logo." I closed three. Then it was "the EAE band is full." It's always something.

I don't think he was lying to me. I think he genuinely wanted to promote me and also didn't have the political will to fight for the comp band with the VP. Either way the effect on me was the same.

The frustrating part is that I was happy here otherwise. My customers are great. My SE partner is phenomenal. Our product sells itself in the mid-market. I probably would have stayed five years at Meridian if the promotion conversation had gone differently.

I'm going to a competitor as an Enterprise AE with a $40k OTE bump and a real ramp plan. Comp matters but the signal that they wanted me at that level mattered more.

One thing I'd pass along: there is a pattern in sales where people get promoted by leaving and coming back. Three of my peers did exactly that. It's not a great look for retention. I would not want my CHRO to be blind to that.
