Exit Interview — Meridian Technologies
Interview ID: EXIT-0028
Date: January 2025
Function: Customer Success
Role: Customer Success Manager, Senior
Location: Remote-US
Tenure: 3 years 2 months
Departure type: Voluntary

Notes:

I'm moving from CS into a Product role at a smaller company. Classic CSM-to-PM pivot. I tried to do this internally at Meridian (I know, another one of these stories) and couldn't, so I'm doing it externally.

What I want to add beyond the usual CSM-to-PM story: the new role is at a company that was previously a Meridian customer. I was their CSM here for two years. I know their product challenges from the inside of the relationship. They offered me the role explicitly because I already understood what they were trying to build.

That's an interesting data point. Meridian CSMs are sitting on deep product knowledge about their customers' businesses. Our customers recognize this. Meridian doesn't seem to.

A suggestion for anyone reading: create an explicit "CSM to PM" pipeline and sell it in CS hiring. I would have stayed a few more years for a clear path. Multiple CSMs I've talked to would have too.

Other comments:

- My time at Meridian has been mostly positive. The product is great to sell. The CS org has talented people. My managers have been solid.
- CS comp structure has gotten behind market in the last year, but that's not the reason I'm leaving. It's the path, not the pay.
- The renewal and expansion revenue I drove at Meridian will speak for itself on my résumé. I'm grateful for the trust I was given on those accounts.

Thanks for three years. I'll be rooting for the CSMs I leave behind.
