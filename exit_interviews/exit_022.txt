Exit Interview — Meridian Technologies
Interview ID: EXIT-0022
Date: December 2024
Function: Customer Success
Role: Customer Success Manager
Location: Bangalore
Tenure: 2 years 1 month
Departure type: Voluntary

Notes:

My spouse works in Mumbai and we've been long-distance for most of my two years at Meridian. He couldn't relocate because his family business is there. I was accepting that arrangement because the career trajectory was worth it.

Meridian offered me a London relocation opportunity earlier this year. I was excited initially and then realized that relocating farther from Mumbai would make the long-distance situation much worse. I declined. After that I started thinking more seriously about what I wanted my life to look like.

I've accepted a role at a company in Mumbai. I'll be 2 hours from my husband instead of an 11-hour flight. That's the answer.

This isn't a Meridian complaint. The CS work I did here was meaningful and I learned a lot. My manager was good. My team in Bangalore was collegial. The London opportunity was generous — it just didn't fit my life.

Feedback I'd offer:

- When offering international relocation, ask the person earlier what their personal constraints are. I could have told you in a 20-minute conversation that London wasn't going to work for me, before we spent a month on logistics.
- Our Bangalore CS team has a few folks with spouses in other cities. Worth mapping.
- The India CS market has heated up significantly. Our comp is behind where it needs to be to retain senior CSMs.

Thanks for two years. I would work for Meridian again in the right circumstances.
