Exit Interview — Meridian Technologies
Interview ID: EXIT-0017
Date: November 2024
Function: Sales
Role: Account Executive, Senior
Location: NYC
Tenure: 2 years 2 months
Departure type: Voluntary

Notes:

Sales quota pressure has gotten to me. I'm leaving for a role that is less intense and I'll sleep better.

I hit quota both years I've been here. I've worked 55-60 hour weeks to do it. The last quarter my manager started layering on extra pipeline activities — "outbound days," "pipeline reviews," additional competitive deals — on top of my existing book. I told her I was at my ceiling already. Her response was that we needed to "stretch" and she believed in me. This is a script sales managers everywhere use to keep you from saying no.

I have a medical condition I've been managing for years. Stress makes it worse. Last summer I had a serious flare-up that put me in the hospital for three days. I went back to work a week later because we had a big deal at risk. That was a mistake I'm not going to repeat.

I'm taking a role at a smaller SaaS company with a smaller book, a lower quota, and lower OTE. I'm trading $40k a year for my health. Easy call.

Feedback specifically for sales leadership:
- When reps are consistently hitting quota, adding more pipeline work doesn't increase revenue proportionally. It increases burnout.
- We've had three AEs on medical leave in the last 18 months from the NYC team. That's a lot for a team of 12.
- The "stretch culture" language is fine in an all-hands speech. In 1:1s it reads as pressure and it has consequences.

The product is genuinely great. The customers are interesting. The comp is fine. The pace is the problem.

Thank you for the two years.
