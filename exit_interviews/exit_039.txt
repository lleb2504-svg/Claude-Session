Exit Interview — Meridian Technologies
Interview ID: EXIT-0039
Date: March 2025
Function: People
Role: People Business Partner, Senior
Location: NYC
Tenure: 4 years 1 month
Departure type: Voluntary

Notes:

I'm joining a national education nonprofit as their head of people. This is a career shift I've been thinking about for two years and the right opening came up at the right time.

I want to use this interview for something slightly unusual. As someone who has sat on the other side of many of these conversations, I'd like to offer some aggregated observations about what I've seen from employees leaving Meridian during my tenure.

Themes I've seen across exits:

1. Manager quality is the single biggest driver, good and bad. Great managers retain through hard times; bad managers lose good people during good times. Invest in manager coaching.

2. The internal-mobility process is clunky. People leaving to go "somewhere they can grow" almost always tried to move internally first and couldn't. Fix the lateral-move pipeline.

3. Comp transparency varies by function. Engineering is the most transparent. Design and marketing are the least. The correlation with attrition is real.

4. Acquired employees are a retention risk that doesn't show up cleanly in dashboards because they're folded into general attrition. Look at that cohort specifically.

5. Parental leave and graduated return-to-work would cost less than retention marketing and buy more loyalty.

For my own exit specifically: this is a pull not a push. The nonprofit mission aligns with something personal for me. I've had a great experience at Meridian and I'll miss the work and the people.

If I can help as an alum with anything specific — reviews, training, referrals — please ask.
