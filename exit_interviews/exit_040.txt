Exit Interview — Meridian Technologies
Interview ID: EXIT-0040
Date: March 2025
Function: Engineering
Role: Software Engineer, Staff
Location: SF
Tenure: 4 years 0 months
Departure type: Voluntary

Notes:

Offer from a large tech company. Staff-equivalent level, materially higher comp, specifically on the equity side. I'm taking it.

The difference in equity is the thing. Meridian equity is nominal and illiquid. The big-tech equity is publicly traded and pays out every six months. Over a 3-4 year horizon the delta is significant.

I want to note for the record: this was not a push. My manager is good. My team is fine. My work has been meaningful. I've enjoyed four years here. I'd stay if the comp math were comparable.

Feedback:

- Pre-IPO private company equity has become a hard sell to tenured engineers in 2024-2025. The market has been choppy enough that the "trust us, the exit is coming" narrative doesn't land the way it used to. I'd think hard about whether Meridian's retention strategy relies too heavily on equity that employees are discounting heavily.
- Annual refreshes have been inadequate to bridge the gap. My refresh at year three was smaller than I expected given performance.
- An IPO, a tender offer, or a secondary program would change the math substantially. I don't know what's feasible but the status quo is costing retention of tenured senior engineers.
- The team I leave will backfill me. The person they hire will cost more and have less context. Fully-loaded cost of turnover at this level is probably $400K+. Paying me an extra $60K in equity per year for two years would have been the right trade.

Four good years. I'm taking what I learned with me. Thank you.
