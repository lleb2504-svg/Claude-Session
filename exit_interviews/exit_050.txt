Exit Interview — Meridian Technologies
Interview ID: EXIT-0050
Date: May 2025
Function: Operations
Role: Business Operations Manager
Location: SF
Tenure: 3 years 0 months
Departure type: Voluntary

Notes:

I'm going back to school for an MBA. I've been working toward this for a couple of years — applications submitted last fall, admitted in February, start this August.

I want to reflect a bit on why I chose this moment.

What pulled me out: I want to pivot from operations into something closer to general management. I've talked with a few of our execs about whether there's a path to GM from my current role inside Meridian and the honest answer is that there isn't a short one. The GM-equivalent roles here are filled externally or by people coming out of product leadership. My 3 years of ops won't bridge to that track without either a decade of internal patience or an external credential. The MBA is a faster credential.

What I would have stayed for: a rotation into product management. I raised this with my skip-level twice. The second conversation ended with "we don't typically move ops folks into PM." That was honest. And final.

Things I want to share since I'll be an alum:

- Ops at Meridian is strong but the career ladders inside it are short. Most ops people at Meridian plateau at Senior Manager or leave. I don't think that's intentional. I think it's an unexamined legacy of how the function grew.
- The tuition-assistance benefit doesn't cover graduate school beyond a certain cap. If Meridian wants to retain people like me, a more generous graduate-school benefit with a tenure commitment would be interesting.
- I'll consider Meridian for internship next summer and for full-time post-MBA. Keep me on the recruiting list.

Grateful for the three years. Learned a lot. Onto the next thing.
