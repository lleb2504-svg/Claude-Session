Exit Interview — Meridian Technologies
Interview ID: EXIT-0020
Date: December 2024
Function: Sales
Role: Sales Development Representative
Location: Austin
Tenure: 1 year 6 months
Departure type: Voluntary

Notes:

I was told when I was hired that the SDR-to-AE pipeline was strong and that most SDRs promote within 12–15 months. I am at 18 months and I'm not promoting. When I pushed on this with my manager she said the AE ramp program is "paused" due to headcount constraints. Okay. I asked her to put a date on when it resumes. She couldn't.

Without a promotion path I can't stay. SDR base is livable but not long-term livable in Austin. The upside was supposed to come from the promotion to AE, at which point OTE is meaningfully higher and also commissionable in a real way. If that promotion is now 24 months or 30 months or who knows, I need to exit.

I got offered an AE role at another SaaS company that is willing to take me without the AE-I step. It's a smaller company and the territory is going to be a grind. But it's closer to the comp I was expecting at Meridian by now.

Feedback:
- If the SDR-to-AE path is paused, tell people at the top of the funnel so they don't join expecting it.
- Our SDR class of 2023 has lost roughly half of its members to other companies. I doubt that's your target attrition.
- My manager was great. I want that on record. The organizational constraint is the issue, not her.

I had a good experience being an SDR here. I learned a lot. The product sells itself at the prospecting stage, which I appreciated. I just need to earn more to keep paying rent.
