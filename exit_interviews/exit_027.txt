Exit Interview — Meridian Technologies
Interview ID: EXIT-0027
Date: January 2025
Function: Customer Success
Role: Customer Success Manager, Senior
Location: London
Tenure: 4 years 0 months
Departure type: Voluntary

Notes:

Combination of things but the core is work-life balance. Specifically: return-to-office policy, London-specific market conditions, and a CS workload that has grown faster than the team.

RTO: my commute is about 75 minutes each way. Three days a week in office adds 7–8 hours of unpaid commuting to my week. I used to use that time for email and prep work. Now I'm standing in a train and arriving with less focus than when I was fully remote.

Workload: when I joined I had 25 accounts. Now I have 48. Headcount in EMEA CS has grown some but not proportional to book growth. Meridian's product has expanded, which means more modules to support per account, which means more work per account. My book is more than twice what it was.

Renewals used to be stressful one quarter a year. Now they're year-round because we ship new products constantly and every customer interaction is a renewal conversation in disguise.

I have a 7-year-old and a 4-year-old. I have not been home for dinner most nights this year. My partner runs a real career too and the math stopped working for us.

I'm joining a competitor that is fully remote. Base is about the same. The difference is I'll be home at 5:30pm on a Tuesday.

Feedback:
- Book size per CSM in EMEA is too high. You are going to lose a lot of us in 2025 if nothing changes.
- The RTO policy was sold as "flexibility" but the 3-day minimum doesn't feel flexible. A 1-2 day expectation would land very differently.
- The CS team in London is aware of the attrition risk. Leaders above have not acted on it.

Good luck.
