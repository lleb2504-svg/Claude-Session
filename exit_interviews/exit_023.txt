Exit Interview — Meridian Technologies
Interview ID: EXIT-0023
Date: December 2024
Function: Operations
Role: Operations Director
Location: SF
Tenure: 8 years 3 months
Departure type: Voluntary (retirement)

Notes:

I'm retiring. After 35 years in tech, the last 8 of which have been here, I'm done. This isn't a resignation with a twist — I'm not going anywhere else. I'm going to do some light advisory work, spend more time with my grandchildren, and finally take a proper sabbatical.

I wanted to write real notes here because I've done a lot of these interviews from the other side of the table.

Things I think Meridian does well:
- Ops as a function has genuine seats at the executive table here. Not every company does this. It matters.
- The engineering culture, at least in the parts of it I touched, is strong. There's rigor. There's care.
- Leadership has been willing to have hard conversations when they needed to. That wasn't always true in my career.

Things I'd change:
- The Staff-to-Director layer feels under-supported. When I look at the people who've left my team over the years, a lot of them were at that transition. Not all of them should have been promoted, but more of them should have been coached.
- The Bangalore-site strategy has been a work in progress for three years. At some point the decision needs to be made in principle — is it a real hub or a satellite — and then resourced accordingly. We keep splitting the difference.
- Re-org fatigue is real. My org has been restructured five times in eight years. The people who live through each one remember the costs.

I'll miss the work. I'll miss the people more. Thank you for a fine run. Send me the IPO filing when it finally happens.
