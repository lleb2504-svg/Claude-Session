Exit Interview — Meridian Technologies
Interview ID: EXIT-0005
Date: September 2024
Function: Marketing
Role: Marketing Manager, Senior
Location: NYC
Tenure: 4 years 1 month
Departure type: Voluntary

Notes:

Four years of 3% annual increases. That's the whole story on comp. In aggregate I am earning less in real dollars than I was on my start date. Inflation alone has outpaced my raises.

I asked my manager last year for a market adjustment. He went to the VP. The VP said marketing comp was not a priority for the year. I let it go. I asked again this year. Same answer. I concluded that marketing comp is never a priority and went to look elsewhere.

I got an offer at 22% above what I make here. Not amazing by 2021 standards. Reasonable by 2025 standards. Either way a lot more than 3%.

I want to add something about my manager because I've said only positive things so far. He's great. He advocated for me. He tried to help me get promoted to Director but the org chart didn't have room and a director hire was brought in externally at the level I would have been promoted to. That was the other shoe dropping. I realized the career path above me was going to be external hires not internal promotions. I wasn't going to wait another two years to find out I was right.

Feedback that might matter:
- 3% as a default raise feels like a policy from a different era.
- Internal promotions into Director roles in marketing specifically are rare. People notice.
- The new Director is a fine person. It's the pattern I'm flagging.

I'd come back to Meridian if the comp conversation changed. I still believe in the product and the mission.
