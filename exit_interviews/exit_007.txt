I'm stepping back from corporate work to be a primary caregiver for my child for at least a year. My partner and I made this decision jointly and it's been in the works for months.

This isn't a resignation driven by Meridian. I want to be specific. Meridian has been a great employer. My manager has been supportive. The work was interesting. I loved most of the people on my team.

The reason I'm writing this up at length anyway is that I don't want "personal reasons" to hide potentially useful signal. So:

Signal that's about me and not Meridian: I always planned to take a break when we had kids. My partner's career is higher earning right now so economically I'm the one stepping back. This was going to happen at some point.

Signal that might be about Meridian: the parental leave policy in the US is 16 weeks. That's decent but not great compared to what some of my peer companies offer. When I returned from leave last year I felt very rushed. A six-month ramp-back, or a four-day workweek for the first three months post-leave, would have made me more likely to try to stay in some form.

I'd like to be considered for a return if I come back to work. I know that's not always an option but I want it on record. Please keep my info somewhere.

Thank you to the three teams I worked with. I'm genuinely grateful. Tell my manager I appreciated her more than I said.
