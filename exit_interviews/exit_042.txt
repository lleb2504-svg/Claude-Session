Exit Interview — Meridian Technologies
Interview ID: EXIT-0042
Date: April 2025
Function: Customer Success
Role: Customer Success Manager, Senior
Location: London
Tenure: 3 years 1 month
Departure type: Voluntary

Notes:

Three years of 2% increases on an already-below-market CSM base in London. I did the arithmetic last month and realized I'm making about £8K less than the median for my role in London per a couple of comp sites. Even accounting for uncertainty in those benchmarks, the gap is real.

I asked my manager what the ceiling was for CSM-Senior comp in EMEA and she said the UK cost-of-living bands are set globally and there's no local discretion. I get that in principle. In practice it means Meridian's EMEA CSMs are underpaid relative to local market and our attrition reflects it.

I'm joining a competitor headquartered in the UK with a CSM comp structure that is actually calibrated to London. £22K more per year. That's real money.

Additional notes:

- The RTO push this year added one more reason to look. I don't mind being in office two days but my commute is 75 minutes each way and that's 3+ unpaid hours on an office day that used to be working time.
- I have loved my accounts. I have great relationships with several of them. I will personally email them with a handoff and an introduction to my replacement — with your permission.
- The CS leadership in EMEA has had a lot of turnover in the last 18 months. I've had three managers. I'd flag that as a destabilizing force for the team.

I understand that global comp structures are not easy to change. But they're the reason I'm leaving. I would stay if London comp were benchmarked to London, and I think half of my team would too.
