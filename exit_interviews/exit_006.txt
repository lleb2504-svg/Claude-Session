Exit Interview — Meridian Technologies
Interview ID: EXIT-0006
Date: September 2024
Function: Design
Role: Product Designer, Staff
Location: Remote-US
Tenure: 3 years 4 months
Departure type: Voluntary

Notes:

I wanted to move into design management. I made that clear in every 1:1 from year two onward. Meridian does not have a path from senior-IC design into design management, or at least not a visible one. When a design manager slot opened last year it was filled externally.

I talked to the person who got hired. She's lovely and qualified. My issue is not with her individually. My issue is that I've been told "we hire managers from outside because we want fresh perspective" and that's fine as a strategy but it means no one on the IC track can plan for a move into management. You either wait forever or you go somewhere else.

I'm going somewhere else. I'm taking a Design Manager role at a smaller company that's building out their design org. I'll have four direct reports on day one. Even if the startup fails in 18 months I'll have real management experience to come back with.

Other feedback:

- Design IC growth at Meridian is solid. I got good feedback at every review. I don't have complaints about my IC career.
- Design management growth is the gap. We lose senior ICs to competitors because they can't stay and grow into the management role they want.
- If you want to fix this, commit to filling at least half of design manager openings internally for the next two years. That signal alone will change behavior.

I've enjoyed my time here. I've worked on three products I'm genuinely proud of. The design org leadership knows how I feel — I've told them twice. Nothing changed.
